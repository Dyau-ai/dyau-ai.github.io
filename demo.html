How Your Invoices Flow to LHDN MyInvois

A beginner-friendly view of how data seamlessly travels from your billing system through the Dyau.ai platform to official government clearance and back.

1

Invoice Data Arrives

Received from your billing, ERP, or accounting software.

2

Safe Storage

Encrypted and safely logged in the compliant platform.

3

Pre-Validation Check

Platform checks formatting, tax rates, and buyer/seller data.

4

Document Submitted

Securely transmitted to the official LHDN MyInvois portal.

5

LHDN Validates

The tax authority validates business rules and tax identity.

6

Result Returned

LHDN returns unique UUID and official validation status.

7

Sent to Source System

UUID, QR link, and verified status synced back to your ERP.

Deliverables

What the Customer Receives Back

Upon every submission, your source accounting and ERP systems automatically receive four complete verification assets:

🔑

Unique Identifier (UUID)

A globally unique reference number generated by LHDN that officially marks the document as recognized in national tax records.

✅

Validation Status

Clear, unambiguous status indication (Valid, On Hold, Rejected, Cancelled) with structured audit details for finance operations.

⏱️

Official Timestamp

Official government clearance timestamp confirming the exact legal second of compliance acknowledgement.

📱

QR / Validation Link

Embeddable QR graphic and direct web verification URL ready to be stamped onto customer-facing PDF invoices and printouts.
